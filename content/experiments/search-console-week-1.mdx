This experiment series documents the early Search Console journey for Pretty Fly for a Website. Week 1 is about setup and baseline measurement, not conclusions.

<FlowDiagram steps="Verify property | Submit sitemap | Record indexed pages | Record queries | Wait for enough data" />

## Hypothesis

Fresh educational content should begin earning discovery signals before it earns meaningful clicks. The first useful milestone is not traffic. It is reliable visibility data.

## Methodology

We will record indexed pages, impressions, clicks, average position, query variety, and crawl/indexing issues once a week. We will not treat early noise as proof.

## What we are measuring

- Pages discovered and indexed
- Queries appearing in Search Console
- Impressions by page
- Clicks by page
- Average position ranges
- Technical warnings or indexing blockers
