This experiment asks a narrow question: can one relevant backlink change measurable search behavior for a specific page? It will not claim one link guarantees rankings.

<FlowDiagram steps="Choose target page | Record baseline | Earn relevant link | Wait | Compare query and position changes" />

## Hypothesis

One relevant backlink may help discovery or confidence for a page, but the effect may be small, delayed, or impossible to isolate from other changes.

## Methodology

We will choose one page, record baseline Search Console data, document the backlink context, avoid major page changes during the measurement window, and compare visibility afterward.

## What we are measuring

- Baseline impressions and clicks
- Average position range
- Query variety
- Crawl timing after the link
- Changes after the measurement window
