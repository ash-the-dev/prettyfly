Week 2 looks for the first signs that Google understands the site. This is where it is tempting to overreact, so the method matters more than the numbers.

<FlowDiagram steps="Collect early impressions | Group by page | Group by query | Flag weird matches | Avoid premature edits" />

## Hypothesis

Early impressions may appear before clicks, and that can be good. It means pages are being tested for relevance.

## Methodology

We will compare week-over-week impressions, note which pages receive query exposure, and avoid making major content changes unless a technical issue is obvious.

## What we are measuring

- Query variety
- Pages with first impressions
- Average position ranges
- Queries that do not match page intent
- Pages with zero-click impressions
