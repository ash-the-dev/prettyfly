Week 3 is about resisting the myth that low positions are automatically bad. A position around 39 can be a clue that Google has started testing a page for a relevant query.

<FlowDiagram steps="Find low-position queries | Check relevance | Match to page intent | Improve internal links | Wait before declaring success" />

## Hypothesis

For a new editorial site, low-position impressions can be a healthy sign when the query is relevant and the page clearly answers the topic.

## Methodology

We will review low-position queries, separate relevant from irrelevant matches, and document whether internal linking or title clarity should be improved.

## What we are measuring

- Relevant low-position queries
- Irrelevant query matches
- Pages gaining visibility across multiple searches
- Internal link opportunities
- Whether edits are justified by patterns

This experiment remains noindex while the evidence is incomplete.
