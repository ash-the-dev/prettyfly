This experiment will track what changes after fixing technical SEO issues such as broken canonicals, missing metadata, crawl blockers, redirect mistakes, or sitemap problems.

<FlowDiagram steps="Find issue | Record baseline | Fix issue | Request recrawl | Compare signals later" />

## Hypothesis

Fixing technical SEO issues should improve crawlability and clarity, but it does not guarantee higher rankings. The effect depends on the issue, the page, and whether the content deserves visibility.

## Methodology

We will document the exact issue, record baseline Search Console and crawl data, apply the fix, and compare the next measurement window.

## What we are measuring

- Crawl and indexing changes
- Error reduction
- Impressions and clicks
- Average position context
- Whether affected pages become easier to discover
