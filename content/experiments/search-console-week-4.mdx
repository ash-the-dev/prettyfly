Week 4 is where the site stops merely observing and starts choosing small, measurable changes. The goal is to make a few useful edits, not rewrite the site because a graph wiggled.

<FlowDiagram steps="Review four weeks | Choose one pattern | Make one edit | Record the change | Measure the next window" />

## Hypothesis

Small, targeted improvements to titles, internal links, and answer quality should be easier to evaluate than broad rewrites.

## Methodology

We will pick a small number of pages with meaningful impressions, document the reason for each change, and review the next measurement window before making another round of edits.

## What we are measuring

- Pages with enough impressions to inspect
- Query intent alignment
- Title and description opportunities
- Internal links added
- Follow-up performance window
