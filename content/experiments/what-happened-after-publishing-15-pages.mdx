This experiment will track what happens after publishing a focused set of helpful pages. It will not invent traffic numbers or pretend early impressions are a guaranteed trend.

<FlowDiagram steps="Publish useful pages | Submit sitemap | Track indexing | Track impressions | Review useful clicks" />

## Hypothesis

A focused content cluster should create more discovery opportunities than isolated one-off posts, provided the pages answer real questions and link to each other naturally.

## Methodology

We will publish the pages, keep a change log, monitor indexing, record Search Console impressions and clicks, and review whether pages support each other through internal links.

## What we are measuring

- Indexed page count
- Impressions by guide
- Clicks by guide
- Query variety
- Internal links between related pages

## Expected results window

The page is noindex while the experiment is in progress. It becomes indexable only after meaningful findings are added.
